Tbilisi, Georgia / Independent community & event series

More thansound.

Modular Friends is a Tbilisi-based community and recurring event series bringing together modular synthesis, experimental live improvisation, and collaborations with visual artists.

17 May 2026

Modular Friends 011 — Synthesia Lab

Announced as Modular Friends 011 at Synthesia Lab, featuring modular synthesis, live improvisation and audiovisual collaborations.

Performers: Neroli Kid / Giga Baindurashvili; William Tavadze; Lado Barnovi; Vaska; TeTe / Tetso / Vazha Marr; Masa Trippin / Shota / Wrong Fingers; AGAVA / Wayward Atom / Spilled Ambiences; Eddie Strxm; jam guests Marching Dog and Tamarma.

Visual artists: Skywalker; TusY; Progenol; Nenealism / Jamanita / Yoyo; She nocturne / Sophie Gvelkuashvili.

Modular Friends at Jazz Club 1984
