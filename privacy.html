MAILING LIST
& PRIVACY.

Purpose. Modular Friends plans an optional mailing list for all significant project updates: live performances, festivals, workshops, lectures, community events, YouTube videos, new music and releases on Bandcamp and Spotify, new merchandise, collaborations and announcements. We do not require newsletter subscription to browse the website.

What information is collected?

Direct email subscription on this site is not yet active. When enabled, the sign-up form will ask for your email address and record your choice to receive Modular Friends updates using a dedicated newsletter provider. The website currently does not collect or save email sign-ups.

Consent and emails

Once our direct sign-up opens, submitting the form and confirming the email verification link will opt you in to Modular Friends email updates. A clear unsubscribe link will be included in future newsletters. Registering for an individual event is separate from email-list consent.

Service provider

Our event calendar is separately hosted by Luma. We intend to use Brevo to collect and manage email subscriptions once our form is activated. Our current site does not transfer signup submissions to Brevo. For calendar privacy details see Luma’s privacy policy; for Brevo see Brevo’s privacy policy.

Retention and requests

Subscription data should be kept while you remain subscribed and then removed or suppressed as required for unsubscribe compliance. For privacy questions, access requests, corrections or deletion requests, contact Modular Friends at mfmodularfriends@gmail.com. Our official Instagram is @modular_friends.

No sale of mailing-list information. The newsletter list is intended solely for Modular Friends communications and is not offered for sale.
