& PRIVACY.

Purpose. Modular Friends offers an optional mailing list for news about live performances, workshops, festivals, lectures, listening events and other activities. We do not require newsletter subscription to browse the website.

What information is collected?

The website links to the Modular Friends calendar on Luma. Following the calendar requires signing in or creating a Luma account. Luma handles the information you provide there. This website does not store subscription submissions.

Consent and emails

Choose Follow on our Luma calendar if you want to receive Modular Friends updates. Manage your email preferences or unfollow through Luma. Event registration is separate from following the calendar.

Service provider

Calendar follows and related email updates are managed through Luma. See Luma’s privacy policy.

Retention and requests

Subscription data should be kept while you remain subscribed and then removed or suppressed as required for unsubscribe compliance. For privacy questions, access requests, corrections or deletion requests, contact Modular Friends at mfmodularfriends@gmail.com. Our official Instagram is @modular_friends.

No sale of mailing-list information. The newsletter list is intended solely for Modular Friends communications and is not offered for sale.
