& PRIVACY.

Purpose. Modular Friends offers an optional mailing list for news about live performances, workshops, festivals, lectures, listening events and other activities. We do not require newsletter subscription to browse the website.

What information is collected?

The subscription form requests an email address. Once enabled, the hosted email platform may record the date and time of signup, consent confirmation and technical information necessary to operate the subscription. Do not send sensitive personal information through the form.

Consent and emails

By choosing to subscribe and confirming your email address, you consent to Modular Friends using your address to send newsletters about its activities. We recommend enabling double opt-in confirmation. Each newsletter should include an unsubscribe link. You can withdraw your consent at any time.

Service provider

Newsletter signups and email delivery are planned to be managed by Brevo. Until the sign-up form is activated, Modular Friends does not collect email addresses via this site. Brevo's own privacy terms apply when you use the hosted sign-up form.

Retention and requests

Subscription data should be kept while you remain subscribed and then removed or suppressed as required for unsubscribe compliance. For privacy questions, access requests, corrections or deletion requests, contact Modular Friends at mfmodularfriends@gmail.com. Our official Instagram is @modular_friends.

No sale of mailing-list information. The newsletter list is intended solely for Modular Friends communications and is not offered for sale.
