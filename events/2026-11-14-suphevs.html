Modular Friends @ Suphevs

On Saturday, 14 November, Modular Friends brings an evening of live electronic music, modular synthesis and visual art to Suphevs Bar in Tbilisi.

The programme features individual live sets by Neroli Kid, Giga Baindurashvili, AGAVA, Spilled Ambiences and Wayward Atom. Each artist brings a distinct approach to sound, exploring electronic textures, rhythms and improvisation through live performance.

Visual artists Yoyoyoyo and Manipura contribute the visual programme, bringing sound and image together throughout the evening.
